# Runbook: Rollback

1. Confirm the release reference, incident severity, approval, and rollback
   target.
2. Preserve release, health, alert, and communication evidence before changing
   the deployment.
3. Execute the configured rollback task in the affected environment.
4. Run health, telemetry, and critical business outcome checks.
5. Keep the incident open until the error budget and customer impact stabilize.
6. Record why the release was rolled back and link the corrective actions.
